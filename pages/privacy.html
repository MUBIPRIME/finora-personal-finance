Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Finora is a personal finance management application designed to help users record, organize, and understand their income, expenses, budgets, and financial activity.

2. Information We Collect

Finora may collect information that you provide when creating and using your account, including your name, email address, and financial records that you choose to enter into the application.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to provide the features of Finora, including account authentication, transaction management, budgeting, reports, and personalized financial summaries.

4. Data Storage

Finora uses Firebase services to provide authentication and database functionality. Your account and transaction information may therefore be stored using Firebase infrastructure.

5. Financial Information

Finora is a financial tracking tool. It does not provide professional financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. You are responsible for the financial information you enter into the application.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored through the application. However, no internet service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Third-Party Services

Finora may rely on third-party services such as Firebase and other technologies required to operate the application. These services may process information according to their own policies and terms.

8. Your Choices

You may choose what financial information you enter into Finora. You are also responsible for keeping your account credentials secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as Finora develops or its features change. Any updated version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Finora handles information, please contact the Finora support team.
